This was my project made on Sunbeam!

This was made using HTML, CSS and a little bit of Java!
It's a website that's supposed to motivate you to work!
Write three tasks, and the more tasks you complete , the sun rises helping the villagers!
But if you don't work the villagers won't have a sun to work in... 

And you know what happens when the villagers don't have a sun to work in? 
They don't go to work.
And you know what happens when they don't go to work?
They don't have money.
And when they don't have money,
their kids go hungry.

You want that?
YOU WANT THAT?!
THEN DO YOUR WORK!!!
